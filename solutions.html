The 8-Layer Architecture and 5-Phase Approach

EnerSync deploys its product suite as a single architecture, spanning eight interdependent layers, in five phases that follow logical dependencies rather than an arbitrary schedule.

Architecture

Eight layers, sensors to financials

Each layer consumes data from the layer below it and produces data for the layer above it. No layer functions in isolation.

LayerSystemsFunction
1
Enterprise Core		 EnerSync Core (ERP) Order management, production scheduling, inventory, cost accounting, regrind genealogy, supplier procurement
2
Shop Floor Execution		 EnerSync Execute (MES) Work order dispatch, automated recipe push to machines, cycle tracking, OEE, actuals reporting to ERP
3
Automation & Control		 EnerSync Pulse (SCADA/IIoT) Real-time floor visibility, sensor data ingestion, time-series data lake, edge buffering
4
Quality & Compliance		 EnerSync Quality (QMS) ISO 9001:2015 documentation, NCR/CAPA, SPC, supplier quality, audit trail
5
Intelligence & Optimization		 EnerSync Pulse (AI extensions), EnerSync Insight (BI) Predictive maintenance, AI visual inspection, digital twin simulation, management dashboards
6
Product & Supply Chain		 EnerSync Design (PLM), EnerSync Stock (WMS), EnerSync Supply (SCM) Mold/design version control, parameter profile management, recipe push, warehouse traceability, procurement automation
7
Energy & Sustainability		 EnerSync Energy (EMS), EnerSync Sustain (ESG) Machine-level sub-metering, energy waste identification, Scope 1/2/3 carbon reporting, recycled-content traceability
8
Infrastructure & Security		 IT/OT network, edge servers, identity OT network segmentation, factory-floor DMZ, passive traffic monitoring, unified identity management

Implementation

Five phases, each a foundation for the next

BI dashboards are worthless without data from MES and ERP. Predictive maintenance cannot be accurate without months of sensor history. Every phase below builds the foundation the next phase depends on — and delivers concrete operational value on its own before the next begins.

  1. 1

    Before the first production run

    Protect & See

    The factory floor network is fully isolated from corporate IT before anything else is connected, with all cloud traffic routed through a controlled, monitored DMZ. EnerSync Pulse connects to every machine via OPC-UA and begins collecting machine state, alarms, sensor readings, and energy draw from the moment it goes live, buffered on ruggedized edge servers that keep production running through a cloud outage. This phase has no dependency on any other EnerSync product — it goes live as soon as machines are installed.

    IT/OT segmentationEnerSync PulseEdge servers
  2. 2

    Business under control

    Run

    EnerSync Core records every order, generates the production schedule, tracks inventory, calculates batch cost, and manages supplier purchase orders — monitoring stock in real time and raising purchase orders before shortage thresholds are reached. EnerSync Execute translates schedules into machine-level action, pushing production parameters directly to machine controllers and eliminating manual, error-prone parameter entry at every shift change.

    EnerSync CoreEnerSync Execute
  3. 3

    Quality credentials, visibility, efficiency

    Measure

    EnerSync Quality manages every process required for ISO 9001:2015 certification and automatically opens non-conformance records when production deviates beyond acceptable limits. EnerSync Pulse extends into full IIoT data collection across all machines and utilities, building the sensor history Phase 4 depends on. EnerSync Energy sub-meters every major circuit to identify waste. EnerSync Stock completes the material traceability chain. EnerSync Insight consolidates every product's data into near-real-time management dashboards.

    EnerSync QualityEnerSync Pulse (extended)EnerSync EnergyEnerSync StockEnerSync Insight
  4. 4

    Machine intelligence and automated quality

    Optimize

    Predictive maintenance models activate on the sensor baseline accumulated since Phase 3, learning normal operating signatures and detecting early signs of wear weeks before failure. AI visual inspection scores every unit against a defect library and logs results to EnerSync Quality with image evidence. Digital twin simulation lets engineers optimize process parameters virtually before physical trials. EnerSync Design becomes the authoritative, version-controlled repository for designs and process profiles, with approved changes flowing automatically to the shop floor.

    Predictive maintenanceAI visual inspectionDigital twinEnerSync Design
  5. 5

    Multi-market commercial and sustainability infrastructure

    Scale

    EnerSync Supply automates procurement at scale, reading inventory from Stock and forecasts from Core to trigger purchase orders before reorder thresholds. EnerSync Connect structures the commercial side of the business as customer relationships expand across markets. EnerSync Sustain draws data from Energy, Core, Stock, and Supply to produce Scope 1, 2, and 3 carbon footprint reports and recycled-content traceability documentation for buyers with sustainability requirements.

    EnerSync SupplyEnerSync ConnectEnerSync Sustain
