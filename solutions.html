Equipment and Software, Delivered Together

EnerSync supplies the hardware a renewable plant or grid runs on and the software that operates it — architected as four layers and brought online in five phases that follow real dependencies.

Architecture

Four layers, field device to market

Each layer consumes data from the layer below it and produces decisions for the layer above it. The equipment we trade sits at the bottom of the stack; the software suite runs on top of it.

Implementation

Five phases, each a foundation for the next

Forecasts are only as good as the measured history behind them. Battery dispatch needs trustworthy meter data. Carbon reports need both. Every phase below delivers value on its own and builds the foundation the next one depends on.

  1. 1

    Equipment installed and energised

    Connect & See

    Solar panels, inverters, trackers, wind turbines, and switchgear are delivered and installed alongside the SCADA hardware — RTUs, data loggers, and network equipment. EnerSync Shield segments the plant control network before anything is exposed, and EnerSync GridView connects every device from commissioning day, giving operators live single-line diagrams, alarms, and a historian from the first kilowatt-hour.

    Panels & invertersTurbinesSCADA hardwareEnerSync GridViewEnerSync Shield
  2. 2

    Energy under control

    Store & Dispatch

    Battery energy storage systems and smart meters go live together. EnerSync Store dispatches the BESS for peak shaving, arbitrage, solar firming, and frequency response while protecting battery health. EnerSync Meter collects and validates interval data from every revenue and check meter, so dispatch decisions and settlement rest on the same trusted numbers.

    BESSSmart metersEnerSync StoreEnerSync Meter
  3. 3

    Generation you can plan around

    Predict & Maintain

    With operating history building since Phase 1, EnerSync Forecast produces intraday and day-ahead generation forecasts for grid scheduling and trading, and feeds them into battery dispatch. EnerSync Assets watches inverters, gearboxes, blades, trackers, and transformers for early signs of degradation, turning alerts into work orders and spare-parts requests — including parts sourced through EnerSync Trading.

    EnerSync ForecastEnerSync AssetsSpare parts supply
  4. 4

    Many assets, one resource

    Orchestrate

    Microgrid controllers, transformers, and distribution switchgear extend the system to the point of grid connection. EnerSync Flex supervises islanding and reconnection, enforces grid-code requirements for power, voltage, and ramp rates, and aggregates solar, wind, and storage sites into a virtual power plant that can respond to operator setpoints and market signals.

    Microgrid controllersTransformersSwitchgearEnerSync Flex
  5. 5

    Proof for investors, lenders, and buyers

    Report & Scale

    EnerSync Carbon converts validated generation into REC / I-REC tracking, Scope 2 carbon reports, and investor and lender reporting packs. Because every layer is already standardised, each additional site joins the portfolio with the same equipment specifications, the same integrations, and the same reporting from day one.
