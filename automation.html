Automation

Product-line automation systems and IoT deployments that connect machines, sensors, and controllers directly into the same data layer EnerSync Pulse depends on.

Automation Division

Systems we design, supply, and commission

Where the EnerSync product suite makes production data visible and actionable, this division supplies and integrates the physical automation and sensing layer that data comes from.

Product Line Systems

Conveyor and material handling, assembly-line automation, machine vision inspection, and line controllers for end-to-end production lines.

  • Conveyor, AGV, and material-handling integration
  • Assembly automation with collaborative robots and error-proofing
  • Machine vision inspection feeding quality data upstream
  • PLC-based line control with OEE monitoring at the station level

IoT Systems

Sensor-to-cloud architectures — sensors, edge gateways, and connectivity that extend a factory's data footprint beyond what machine controllers alone report.

  • Vibration, temperature, flow, and energy sensor deployment
  • Edge gateways with protocol conversion for legacy equipment
  • Wireless sensor networks for large or hard-to-wire facilities
  • Edge AI compute for on-site inference and anomaly detection

Pairs With the Product Suite

One data layer, from sensor to dashboard

Automation and IoT systems supplied through this division feed directly into EnerSync Pulse (SCADA/IIoT) and, from there, into EnerSync Insight for dashboards and EnerSync Pulse's predictive maintenance models — the same architecture described on the Solutions page.

Sensor coverage matched to Phase 1

Automation Division deployments are scoped against the sensor coverage EnerSync Pulse needs for Phase 1 (Protect & See), so the physical layer and the software layer are never designed in isolation.

A foundation for Phase 4

The IoT sensor history this division helps build becomes the baseline predictive maintenance models need in Phase 4 (Optimize) — see the Product Suite for how that phase works.

Ready to automate a line or extend your sensor coverage?

EnerSync automation engineers can assess your process and design a system that reports directly into your existing or planned EnerSync Pulse deployment.
