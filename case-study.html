Reference Architecture: 50 MWp Solar + 20 MWh BESS Hybrid Plant

An example design showing how EnerSync equipment and software fit together in a typical solar-plus-storage plant.

This is an illustrative design, not a client project

The plant size, equipment and phasing below are a typical example to show how the pieces connect. Every real project is scoped to its own site, grid code and offtake agreement.

The Example Plant

What the design assumes

A ground-mounted 50 MWp PV plant on single-axis trackers, with a 20 MWh battery storage system at the same site. Both connect to the grid through an on-site substation. The battery is designed to store midday solar output, smooth ramp rates and shift energy into evening peak hours.

50 MWp PV capacity on single-axis trackers
20 MWh containerised battery storage
5 deployment phases

Rollout

Equipment and software by phase

Each phase lists the traded equipment and the EnerSync software products it would typically involve.

  1. 1

    Connect & See

    Plant monitoring and a secure control network

    Equipment: PV modules, inverters, trackers, DC cabling and MC4 connectors, transformers, MV switchgear, plant SCADA hardware.
    Software: EnerSync GridView collects data from inverters, trackers, meters and protection relays. EnerSync Shield separates the plant control network from corporate IT and monitors it from before energisation.

  2. 2

    Store & Dispatch

    Battery storage and revenue metering

    Equipment: battery containers, power conversion systems (PCS), battery management systems (BMS), smart meters.
    Software: EnerSync Store schedules charging and discharging and manages state of charge. EnerSync Meter collects revenue-grade meter data for settlement.

  3. 3

    Predict & Maintain

    Forecasting and asset performance

    Equipment: weather stations and irradiance sensors.
    Software: EnerSync Forecast produces day-ahead and intraday generation forecasts. EnerSync Assets tracks inverter, tracker and battery health and schedules maintenance work orders.

  4. 4

    Orchestrate

    Coordinated dispatch across solar and storage

    Equipment: microgrid controller at the point of connection.
    Software: EnerSync Flex coordinates PV and battery output against grid limits and market or tariff signals, and can enrol the plant in a virtual power plant (VPP) programme.

  5. 5

    Report & Scale

    Sustainability reporting and portfolio growth

    Software: EnerSync Carbon turns metered generation into emissions-avoided reports and supports renewable energy certificate (REC) tracking. Further sites can be added to the same GridView and Flex setup.
