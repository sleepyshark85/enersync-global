The EnerSync Software Suite

Eight enterprise products for solar, wind, battery storage, and power distribution assets — each mapped to one of five deployment phases. Browse by phase to see what goes live, when, and why.

From commissioning day

Phase 1 — Connect & See

Every inverter, turbine, battery rack, and protection relay is connected, secured, and visible from the moment the site is energised.

ProductCategoryFunction
EnerSync GridView Renewable SCADA Plant monitoring and control for solar, wind, and BESS sites. Native drivers for IEC 61850, Modbus TCP/RTU, DNP3, and IEC 60870-5-104 connect inverters, turbine controllers, BMS, meters, and relays. Real-time single-line diagrams, alarm management, and a high-resolution historian that every other EnerSync product reads from.
EnerSync Shield OT Cybersecurity Security designed for energy assets, aligned with IEC 62443. Network zoning and segmentation between plant control and corporate IT, passive OT asset inventory, anomaly detection on industrial protocols, and audited secure remote access for OEMs and O&M contractors.

Energy under control

Phase 2 — Store & Dispatch

Battery storage is dispatched against tariffs and grid signals, and metered energy is validated end to end — from the meter to the bill.

ProductCategoryFunction
EnerSync Store BESS EMS Energy management system for battery storage: dispatch scheduling, peak shaving, energy arbitrage, solar firming, and frequency response. Tracks state of charge and state of health per rack, and applies degradation-aware control so revenue stacking never comes at the cost of warranty limits.
EnerSync Meter AMI / MDM AMI head-end and meter data management for smart meters across plants, substations, and distribution networks. Automated collection, validation, estimation and editing (VEE), interval data storage, and integration with billing and settlement systems.

Generation you can plan around

Phase 3 — Predict & Maintain

With operating history accumulated since Phase 1, forecasting and predictive maintenance models turn plant data into schedules and work orders.

ProductCategoryFunction
EnerSync Forecast AI Forecasting Machine-learning solar and wind generation forecasts combining numerical weather prediction, satellite irradiance, and on-site measurements. Intraday and day-ahead horizons for grid scheduling, curtailment planning, and energy trading, with forecasts fed directly into EnerSync Store dispatch.
EnerSync Assets APM / CMMS Asset performance management and O&M. Predictive models detect early degradation in inverters, turbine gearboxes and blades, trackers, and transformers; work orders, preventive maintenance plans, and spare-parts inventory are managed in one place, linked to the equipment supplied through EnerSync Trading.

Many assets, one controllable resource

Phase 4 — Orchestrate

Distributed generation, storage, and flexible loads are coordinated as a single resource — at a microgrid, across a portfolio, or for the grid operator.

ProductCategoryFunction
EnerSync Flex DERMS / VPP Distributed energy resource management: supervision of microgrid controllers, islanding and reconnection sequences, and virtual power plant aggregation of solar, wind, and BESS sites. Enforces grid-code requirements for active/reactive power and ramp rates, and responds to operator setpoints and market signals.

Proof for investors, lenders, and buyers

Phase 5 — Report & Scale

Metered, validated generation data becomes certificates, carbon reports, and portfolio reporting — ready for each new site that joins.

ProductCategoryFunction
EnerSync Carbon ESG & Certificates Tracks renewable energy certificates (REC / I-REC) against metered generation, produces Scope 2 carbon reports, and generates investor and lender reporting packs from the same audited data used by GridView and Meter.

Each EnerSync product can be licensed on its own or as part of a phased rollout, with implementation, configuration, and go-live support included and annual maintenance and support available thereafter. Pricing is tailored to site count, capacity, and phase scope. Request a formal quotation for exact figures.
