The EnerSync Product Suite

Eleven products, each mapped to one of the five deployment phases. Browse by phase to see what goes live, when, and why.

Before the first production run

Phase 1 — Protect & See

Network segmentation and real-time floor visibility, live before machines produce their first unit.

ProductCategoryFunction
EnerSync Pulse SCADA / IIoT Connects to every machine via OPC-UA and begins real-time collection of machine state, alarms, sensor readings, cycle counters, and energy draw, buffered locally on edge servers and stored in a time-series data lake for use by MES, BI, and predictive maintenance in later phases.

Business under control

Phase 2 — Run

ERP and MES go live together, eliminating manual parameter entry and giving management real-time visibility into orders, schedules, and production.

ProductCategoryFunction
EnerSync Core ERP Order management, production scheduling, inventory monitoring, cost accounting for every batch, supplier purchase orders, and regrind genealogy tracking from Day 1.
EnerSync Execute MES Translates production schedules into machine-level action: work order dispatch, automated recipe push to machine controllers, cycle tracking, OEE, downtime capture, and actuals reporting back to ERP.

Quality credentials, visibility, efficiency

Phase 3 — Measure

Quality, energy, warehouse, and dashboard products bring certification readiness and management-level visibility to the operation.

ProductCategoryFunction
EnerSync Quality QMS ISO 9001:2015 documentation, non-conformance recording, CAPA, supplier quality tracking, and internal audit scheduling. Automatically opens NCRs from Execute when a production cycle deviates from limits.
EnerSync Energy EMS Machine-level and circuit-level sub-metering at 15-minute intervals, identifying waste by machine, circuit, and time of day. Supports ISO 50001 reporting.
EnerSync Stock WMS Records every material delivery, every issue to production, and every regrind batch returned from the crusher with full source traceability, completing the regrind genealogy chain.
EnerSync Insight BI Consolidates data from every EnerSync product into near-real-time management dashboards: OEE by machine, first-pass yield, scrap rate, energy cost per kilogram, on-time delivery, and cost per unit.

Machine intelligence and automated quality

Phase 4 — Optimize

Predictive maintenance, AI inspection, digital twin, and version-controlled design become active on the sensor baseline built since Phase 1.

ProductCategoryFunction
EnerSync Pulse
(AI extensions)		 SCADA / IIoT Predictive maintenance models activate on 6–12 months of accumulated sensor history, learning normal operating signatures and detecting early wear signatures before failure. Also supports digital twin simulation of process parameters.
EnerSync Design PLM The single authoritative, version-controlled repository for mold drawings and process parameter profiles, linked to the machine recipes they apply to. Approved design changes flow automatically to Execute.

Multi-market commercial and sustainability infrastructure

Phase 5 — Scale

Procurement automation, structured commercial management, and sustainability reporting support growth across multiple markets and customers.

ProductCategoryFunction
EnerSync Supply SCM Reads inventory levels from Stock, applies production forecasts from Core, and triggers purchase orders automatically as inventory approaches reorder thresholds. Includes supplier scorecards.
EnerSync Connect CRM Structures the commercial side of the business: customer accounts, quotation tracking, order history, and sales pipeline visibility across markets, specifications, and credit terms.
EnerSync Sustain ESG Draws energy data from Energy, material consumption from Core, waste records from Stock, and transportation data from Supply to produce Scope 1, 2, and 3 carbon footprint reports and recycled-content traceability documentation.

Each EnerSync product is a one-time purchase — perpetual license, implementation, configuration, and go-live support included, with annual maintenance and support available from Year 2. Pricing is tailored to your production scale and phase scope. Request a formal quotation for exact figures.
