About EnerSync Global

A Singapore-based company supplying renewable energy and power equipment, and building the enterprise software that makes it perform.

Mission

Connecting clean energy equipment with the intelligence to run it

The energy transition is being built from physical equipment: solar panels and inverters, wind turbines, battery storage, transformers, and switchgear. That equipment only delivers its full value when it is monitored, dispatched, maintained, and reported on with the right software. EnerSync Global exists to supply both. Our trading business sources and supplies equipment across solar, wind, battery storage, and power distribution, and our software suite covers SCADA, storage dispatch, metering, forecasting, asset performance, DERMS, and carbon reporting, delivered as one architecture rather than disconnected tools.

Values

What guides how we supply and deploy

Specified together

Equipment and software are scoped as one system, so protocols, data points, and control functions line up before anything is shipped or installed.

Security first, not last

Energy assets are critical infrastructure. OT segmentation and secure access are designed in before plant equipment is connected to any network.

Evidence, not promises

Generation, availability, and carbon claims should be backed by metered, auditable data, because that is what offtakers, lenders, and regulators require.

Approach

How EnerSync scopes and delivers a project

01

Understand the site and the grid

Every engagement starts with the project: capacity, technology mix, grid connection requirements, existing equipment, and the protocols and interfaces that carry the most integration risk.

02

Specify equipment and software together

Equipment is quoted against the project's technical requirements, and software products are mapped to the five deployment phases by dependency: dispatch depends on monitoring, and forecasting and predictive maintenance depend on operating history.

03

Supply, deploy, and hand over

Equipment delivery, software commissioning, and role-based training are coordinated at each phase, so operations, maintenance, and commercial teams can run each system as it comes online.
