Why EnerSync

Equipment and intelligence from one partner

Renewable assets earn their return over decades. The equipment you install and the software that runs it should be chosen together, not stitched together after commissioning.

01

Hardware and software, one scope

Panels, inverters, storage, and switchgear are specified alongside the monitoring and control software that will run them, so integration is designed in from day one.

02

Open, standard protocols

IEC 61850, Modbus, DNP3, and IEC 60870-5-104 support means EnerSync software works with the equipment we supply and the equipment you already own.

03

Security by design

OT network segmentation and secure remote access aligned with IEC 62443 go in before plant assets are connected to anything else.

04

Phased, value at every step

Five sequential deployment phases, from visibility to dispatch to optimization, each delivering operational value before the next begins.
