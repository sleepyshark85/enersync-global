Our Core Strength

Energy Trading

We source and supply equipment for solar, wind, battery storage and power distribution projects, for EPC contractors, developers and utilities.

What We Supply

Four equipment categories

Solar Energy

Equipment for rooftop, ground-mount and floating PV plants.

  • Solar panels / photovoltaic (PV) modules
  • Solar inverters (DC to AC conversion)
  • Mounting and racking systems
  • Solar charge controllers
  • Solar trackers
  • Solar cables and connectors (DC cable, MC4)

Wind Energy

Turbines and the major drivetrain components that go into them.

  • Wind turbines
  • Wind turbine blades
  • Generators
  • Gearboxes

Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)

Battery storage for utility-scale and commercial & industrial (C&I) sites.

  • Containerised and cabinet battery systems
  • Power conversion systems (PCS)
  • Battery management systems (BMS)
  • Ready to connect to EnerSync Store (storage EMS)

Power Distribution & Smart Grid

Equipment that connects generation to the grid and to the people who use the power.

  • Smart meters
  • Transformers
  • MV/LV switchgear
  • SCADA systems
  • Microgrid controllers

Hardware + Software

Works with the EnerSync software suite

Monitoring and control

Inverters, turbines, meters and switchgear can report into EnerSync GridView (SCADA), and battery systems into EnerSync Store (BESS EMS).

Metering and microgrids

Smart meters feed EnerSync Meter (AMI/MDM), and microgrid controllers work with EnerSync Flex (DERMS and microgrid orchestration).
